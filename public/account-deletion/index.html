Fledge account deletion

This page explains how a parent or guardian can request deletion of a Fledge account and the family data linked to that account.

Delete from the app

In the Fledge app, sign in with the parent account and open:

Parent home -> Account and data -> Delete account

Type the confirmation phrase shown in the app and submit the request. Fledge will process deletion of the parent login, family profile, child profiles, linked devices, virtual ledger, goals, allowances, tasks, cash-outs and push tokens.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, send the request from the email address used for the parent account. Include "Fledge account deletion" in the subject.

Email privacy support

Virtual money only

Fledge does not move, hold, transfer, invest or store real money. It only records virtual family balances. Any real-world payment is handled outside the app by the adult or guardian.
